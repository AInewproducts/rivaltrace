# RivalTrace outreach draft kit

Status: draft only — nothing has been sent or published.

## Target qualification

Use a target only when all three are true:

1. The audience includes beauty founders, ecommerce operators, merchandisers, pricing researchers, or competitive-intelligence practitioners.
2. The target already publishes tool, template, or evidence-method resources.
3. RivalTrace can add one specific free asset without asking the editor to repeat unsupported product claims.

## Tracking convention

Add `utm_source`, `utm_medium=outreach`, `utm_campaign=resource_citations`, and a target-specific `utm_content`. Record target, page context, send date, reply, live URL, and follow-up owner. A draft is not a send; a reply is not a backlink; a backlink is not traffic until analytics verifies it.
